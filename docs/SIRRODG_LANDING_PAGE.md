## SECTION 2 — THE PROBLEM (Why Now)

### HEADLINE
**The fish is good. The proof is missing.**

### BODY
Foreign product clears customs faster because the paperwork is easier to present.
American product — caught clean, handled right, domestic all the way —
still gets discounted at the counter when the chain cannot verify origin on demand.

That is not a quality problem. It is a documentation problem.

Executive Order 14276 just made this the policy priority.
The USDA Office of Seafood is funded and operational.
Federal procurement is moving toward verified domestic origin.

The buyers who can prove their chain in 2026 will capture the premium.
The ones who cannot will race imports to the bottom.

**This is the moment to build the proof chain.**

## SECTION 3 — HOW IT WORKS (Four Pillars)

### HEADLINE
**Four stages. One chain. No breaks.**

---

### CARD 1 — SEASIDE
[IMAGE: Vessel deck at harvest — net, water, hands on catch]

**SeaSide**
The catch gets a digital fingerprint at the moment it leaves the water.
Vessel ID. Permit. Gear type. Fishing area. Date and time.
Immutable. Nobody rewrites it later.

---

### CARD 2 — DECKSIDE
[IMAGE: Deckhand sorting catch — species, condition check]

**DeckSide**
Species. Weight. Condition. Handling method.
Every variable that determines quality is captured here,
attached to the same record started at SeaSide.

---

### CARD 3 — DOCKSIDE
[IMAGE: Dock landing — forklift, ice bins, receiving station]

**DockSide**
The fish moves from vessel to plant.
The record moves with it — landing weight, transfer, cold chain, plant intake.
No custody gap. No missing link.

---

### CARD 4 — MARKETSIDE
[IMAGE: Retail seafood counter or wholesale buyer reviewing product]

**MarketSide**
Buyer. Distributor. Shelf. Export filing.
The proof rides the fish all the way to the end consumer.
When the buyer scans the product, they see one uninterrupted story.

---

### INTEGRATION NOTE (for web team — small text under the four cards)
SeaSide and DeckSide are horizontally integrated — same catch event, same vessel, same moment.
DockSide and MarketSide are vertically integrated — the market chain that turns proof into price.
