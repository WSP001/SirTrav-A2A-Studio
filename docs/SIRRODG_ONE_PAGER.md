Domestic seafood is getting beat by paper, not by quality.

- The buyer cannot verify origin fast enough.
- The supply chain loses pricing power.
- The producer loses premium capture.
- The market rewards documentation speed instead of actual catch quality.

The result is a structural disadvantage for domestic product even when the fish is better.
That is a solvable structural problem.

## THE SEATRACE ANSWER

SeaTrace creates a **continuous proof chain from water to buyer**.

### Four Pillars. One Chain. No Breaks.

| Stage | What It Captures | Integration Type |
|-------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **SeaSide** | Harvest event — vessel, permit, gear, area, time | Horizontal → |
| **DeckSide** | Species, weight, condition, handling | → Horizontal |
| **DockSide** | Landing, transfer, plant intake, cold chain | Vertical ↓ |
| **MarketSide** | Buyer, distributor, shelf, premium price signal | ↓ Vertical |

**SeaSide + DeckSide** are horizontally integrated — same catch event, same vessel, same moment.
**DockSide + MarketSide** are vertically integrated — the market chain that turns proof into price.

When the end buyer scans the product, they see one uninterrupted story.

## KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS

SeaTrace is measured by business outcomes, not vanity metrics.

### Capture KPIs
- Catch records created within minutes of harvest
- % of trips with complete vessel / gear / area / weight / condition data
- % of records with no manual correction after first entry
- Time from harvest to validated digital record

### Chain KPIs
- % of lots maintaining continuous ID from capture to market
- % of transfers logged without missing custody events
- % of product scanned at dock, plant, or buyer stage
- Exception rate for missing, duplicate, or late records

### Commercial KPIs
- Premium captured vs non-traceable comparable product
- Buyer conversion rate after traceability proof is shown
- Repeat purchase rate from traceability-enabled buyers
- Reduction in rejected or disputed lots

### Operational KPIs
- Average time to issue a complete traceability packet
- % of records validated without staff intervention
- Number of active users completing workflow correctly
- Cost per fully traceable lot

### Strategic KPIs
- Number of districts, fleets, or processors on the same proof standard
- Number of buyers requiring SeaTrace-compatible proof
- Number of products positioned as verified domestic origin
- Revenue tied directly to traceability-supported premium pricing
